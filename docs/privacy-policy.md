# LinguaPath Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 October 2026_

LinguaPath is a language learning app. We built it so that it works without collecting anything about you.

**What we collect:** nothing. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network access.

**What stays on your device:** your chosen languages, lesson progress, XP, streak and review schedule are saved only in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app or using "Reset all progress" deletes them. They are never sent to us or anyone else.

**Pronunciation:** words are spoken by your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech Services). The app sends only the word being pronounced to that engine on your device; please see your phone's text-to-speech provider for its own privacy policy.

**Children:** the app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
