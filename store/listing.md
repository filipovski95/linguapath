**Full description** (max 4000):

Learn the world's big languages in a few minutes a day, anywhere, even without internet.

LinguaPath teaches Chinese (Mandarin), English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Macedonian, and you can learn any of them from any of the others.

★ BITE-SIZE LESSONS
Each lesson introduces a few new words with pictures and pronunciation, then checks them with quick exercises: choose the meaning, pick the right word, listen and choose, and match the pairs.

★ HEAR EVERY WORD
Tap the speaker to hear native-language pronunciation, at normal or slow speed. Chinese words come with Pinyin and Macedonian with Latin letters, so you know how to say them from day one.

★ REVIEW THAT STICKS
Smart flashcards bring each word back just before you'd forget it (spaced repetition). Words you miss come back sooner; words you know well wait longer.

★ USEFUL FROM THE FIRST DAY
Greetings, numbers, food and drink, family, colors, travel and directions, time and everyday verbs: the words you need for your first conversation or trip.

★ STAY MOTIVATED
Earn XP, keep your daily streak, and watch each unit fill up.

★ PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your phone and works on a plane.

More languages and lessons are on the way.
