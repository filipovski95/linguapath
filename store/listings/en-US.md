**Full description** (1159/4000):

Learn the world's big languages in a few minutes a day, anywhere, even without internet.

Saylark teaches Chinese (Mandarin), English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Macedonian. Pick one course and follow your path, lesson by lesson, with Pip, your friendly guide.

★ BITE-SIZE LESSONS
Each lesson introduces a few new words with pictures and pronunciation, then checks them with quick games: choose the meaning, pick the right word, listen and choose, and match the pairs.

★ HEAR EVERY WORD
Tap the speaker to hear clear pronunciation, at normal or slow speed, and pick the voice you like best. Chinese comes with Pinyin and Macedonian with Latin letters.

★ REVIEW THAT STICKS
Smart flashcards bring each word back just before you'd forget it (spaced repetition).

★ FUN EVERY DAY
Cheerful sounds, combos, confetti and Pip cheering you on. Earn XP, keep your daily streak and unlock each unit.

★ USEFUL FROM DAY ONE
Greetings, numbers, food, family, colors, travel, time and everyday verbs.

★ PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your phone and works on a plane.

More languages and lessons are on the way.
